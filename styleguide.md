# Sendmarc Style Guide (Mock)

Voice: friendly-professional, concise, second-person where helpful.
Tone: authoritative but approachable.
Preferred structure: short intro (50-80 words), H2 sections 150-300 words, concluding CTA.

Do NOT:
- Use competitor phrasing verbatim.
- Make unverifiable factual claims without citation.
- Overuse passive voice.

Examples:
- Preferred opening: "Protecting your emails starts with proper authentication. This guide walks through SPF, DKIM and DMARC with practical steps."
- Avoid: "As stated by [Competitor], ..." unless quoting and attributing.
